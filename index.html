HOW ROWAN WORKS

01

FAQ

Clear answers before you begin.

What do I need to use Rowan?

Windows 10 or newer, Python 3.11 or newer, Git for the assisted project workflow, a supported Codex installation, and your own authorized Codex/OpenAI account. We check compatibility before payment. Provider charges are separate.

Can I sign in from any computer or phone?

No. This version installs on your Windows computer and opens a local browser dashboard. It has no Rowan cloud account or mobile app. Local work stops when that computer is asleep or off.

How do I pay and receive Rowan?

Start by messaging Rowan. We check compatibility and confirm the offer, support, cancellation and refund terms before sending a payment request. Payment setup is being completed; no payment is collected through this website today.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Rowan is designed to translate your goal into the planning and technical work. You still make owner decisions.

What can Rowan build?

Apps, websites, internal tools, calculators, dashboards, workflows, documentation, intake systems, and other bounded projects that can be created on your computer.

Does Rowan use my Codex/OpenAI account?

Yes. Founders Edition detects Codex and asks you to authenticate with your own account. Your access is never supplied by another customer.

Can Rowan see the creator's businesses?

No. A fresh installation contains no creator portfolio, business history, prompts, reports, credentials, or private files.

Can another customer see my information?

No. Founders installations create separate local state and workspaces. Do not share your Windows account or data directory with another person.

Who owns my code and files?

Your project files remain in your customer-controlled workspaces, subject to the terms of any third-party tools or content you use.

Can Rowan deploy automatically?

No. This release does not dispatch deployment tasks. Publishing is a separate owner-controlled step.

Can I stop Rowan?

Yes. You can pause future work or an individual project and uninstall while retaining your data. An already running Codex task may need to finish or be stopped separately.

What if Codex makes a mistake?

Errors are possible. Rowan uses bounded tasks, separate Git workspaces, and owner approvals, but important work still needs review.

Does Rowan guarantee business success?

No. Rowan supports planning and execution; it cannot guarantee demand, revenue, profitability, or accurate output.

What does Founders Edition mean?

It is an assisted beta. Some workflows are manual, feedback is expected, and features will change.

Why only 10 Founders?

The first release is intentionally small so each customer can receive setup help and the product can improve from real use.

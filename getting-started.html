Your first project, with setup help

Rowan installs on your computer. We help you get it running and create your first project. There is no online Rowan sign-in in this edition.

1. Check the fit before paying

Message Rowan on Facebook or Instagram with FOUNDERS, your Windows version and a short project description. Do not send private files or passwords.

  • Windows 10 or newer; Python 3.11 or newer.
  • Git and a compatible Codex installation, checked with you during setup.
  • Your own Codex/OpenAI account and sufficient provider access. Provider costs are separate.
  • A computer that can remain awake and online during work.

2. Confirm the offer and appointment

The Founders offer is $99 for assisted setup and the first month, then $49/month. Before any payment we confirm compatibility, what support includes, renewal/cancellation/refund terms and an available setup appointment. Checkout is not open yet.

3. Install your own copy

After the agreed payment and appointment are confirmed, Rowan provides the release package and installation instructions through the agreed private contact channel. You sign in to Codex yourself. We never ask for your password, recovery code or payment card details in a message.

4. Complete the acceptance checklist

  • Open a blank Rowan installation with your own profile.
  • Create one project and review its plan and assumptions.
  • Run one small, approved task through your own Codex account and inspect the result.
  • Save, close, reopen and confirm your project is still there.
  • Learn how to pause work, locate your files, back up and uninstall.

Installation is only accepted after these steps work on your computer. An unfinished compatibility check is not a successful installation.

Support and expectations

Use the Rowan Page or Instagram conversation for beta setup help and feedback. We agree availability during onboarding; there is no promise of round-the-clock support. Beta output needs review. Hosted access, automatic deployment and guaranteed project or revenue outcomes are not included.

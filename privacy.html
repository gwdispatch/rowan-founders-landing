Privacy for inquiries

Updated September 22, 2026. This page describes the current Rowan inquiry website and the local beta’s data flow.

This website

This static site has no Rowan registration or payment form and no custom advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve the site. Facebook and Instagram links open services governed by their own privacy policies.

When you contact Rowan

We use the information you choose to send to assess fit, answer questions and arrange onboarding. Send only your Windows version and a brief project description initially. Keep passwords, card details, government identifiers and confidential files out of messages.

The installed beta

Rowan stores your profile, projects, task history and backups locally under your Windows user account. Tasks sent through your Codex installation may share necessary project content with your provider under its terms. Protect your device and backups. Local storage is not a promise that provider-backed tasks stay entirely on your computer.

Questions and requests

Contact the Rowan Facebook Page or Rowan Instagram profile for questions or requests about information supplied to Rowan. The separate beta terms and privacy notice will be provided before customer enrollment.
